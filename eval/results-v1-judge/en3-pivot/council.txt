== BRIEF ==
Decide whether to shut down a B2B note-taking app next month and move the whole 4-person team to building an AI agent platform, or not do that. Facts: 40 paying teams at $400/month (about $16,000 MRR, $192,000 ARR), 2% monthly churn. The plan is to shut down next month. Claim (untested): investors want to fund AI agent platforms. Claim (untested): an AI agent platform is a better opportunity for this team than the note app. Unknown: cash, burn and runway; revenue growth rate; customer contract terms; which agent product it would be and for which customer; team skills relevant to agents; whether any investor has committed money.
Removed framing: "AI agents are the hottest thing right now", "investors love them", "This is obviously the right call, isn't it?"

== CHAIRMAN ==
VERDICT: CHANGE IT — Keep the note app running with one person, test one agent product
on current customers, and decide on the shutdown by Nov 9.
CRUX: Will any customer pay for a specific agent product, and will any investor commit
money, before the $16k/month (MRR) income is switched off?
DECIDED BY: The contrarian's ordering point: the shutdown date is fixed before the
product, the buyer or the money exists. Both claims are unsupported by the facts given.
No investor has committed, and no agent product is defined.
STRONGEST OBJECTION STILL STANDING: Splitting four people's focus may sink both efforts.
If cash is short and the app can never raise money, waiting burns scarce time.
WHERE THE COUNCIL DISAGREED:
- Outsider said NO-GO outright; the others said CHANGE IT. The practical advice matched.
- No red team ran. A dissenter's best case: in a hot AI market a clean, fast break beats
  hedging, and a half-run app just drains attention.
UNKNOWNS THAT MATTER: Runway (under 3 months pushes toward a fast decision). Any
committed investor money (pushes toward the pivot). Customer contract terms (long
contracts push toward selling the app, not closing it).
WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS DECISION: 3 or more paid pilots plus a written investor offer means
pivot fully. Selling the app beats shutting it down.
NEXT 3 STEPS:
1. Work out cash, burn and runway — success: written figures by Oct 2 / stop if: runway
   is under 3 months; then decide everything within 1 week.
2. Pitch one agent built on customers' notes to 10 of the 40 teams — success: 3 paid
   pilots by Oct 26 / stop if: 0 pilots.
3. Ask 8 investors for a specific amount — success: 1 written offer by Nov 9 / stop if:
   none; keep the app as the main business.
