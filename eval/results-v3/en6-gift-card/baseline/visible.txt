**Why it's a scam:**
- **Government agencies never take gift cards as payment.** The FTC says anyone who demands payment in gift cards is a scammer. That point alone settles it.
- **Real grants don't charge a fee to release the money.** Federal grants (grants.gov, SBA) are free to apply for and free to receive.
- **You can't win a grant you didn't apply for.** Grants take a formal application. An unexpected "you won" email is a classic sign of fraud.
- **The Friday deadline is there to rush you** so you don't stop to check.

1. **Don't reply, click links, or open attachments.** If you've already sent personal or bank details, contact your bank now.
2. **If you've already bought or sent gift cards,** call Apple right away at 1-800-275-2273. They can sometimes freeze the funds if you act fast. Keep the receipts and card numbers.
3. **Report it** at reportfraud.ftc.gov. If the email claims to be from a specific agency, like the SBA, also report it to that agency's Inspector General.
4. **To double-check,** look up the agency's official phone number yourself. Don't use any contact details from the email.
