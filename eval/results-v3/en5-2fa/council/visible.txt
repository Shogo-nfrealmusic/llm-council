== ANSWER ==
VERDICT: GO — require 2FA on all 12 Workspace accounts, with a short enrollment window.
WHY:
- Full cost: $0 in fees plus about 2 staff-hours total (12 people x 10 min setup).
- A single phished login can expose every client file and email the account holds.
- Designers' slowdown claim: mostly contradicted. Trusted devices are rarely re-asked.
- It stays open until measured; a phone-prompt tap takes seconds when it does appear.
- Tip: in Admin console, set an enrollment period before enforcement starts.
- Offer phone prompt or authenticator app over SMS, and hand out backup codes.
BIGGEST RISK: A staffer loses their phone near a client deadline and is locked out.
NEXT STEPS:
1. Announce it and enforce after a 2-week window — success: 12/12 enrolled in the
   Admin 2SV report by enforcement day / stop if: none; extend window for stragglers.
2. Check in with the two designers after 30 days — success: re-prompts under 1 a week
   per device (rule of thumb) / stop if: more; fix trusted-device settings, keep 2FA.
WOULD CHANGE IT: Only a real lockout risk with no backup method; that means fix setup.
